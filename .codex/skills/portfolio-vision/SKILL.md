# Karbyshev.design vision

The portfolio is a digital product, not a screen gallery: it should convey
**Product Designer → Product Thinker → Product Builder**. It targets design and
product leaders, founders, recruiters, and clients. Awwwards-level craft is an
ambition, never a reason to weaken comprehension, evidence, accessibility, or
speed.

Read before visual decisions: `src/app/page.tsx`, `src/app/globals.css`, and
the component being changed. Apply `$awwwards` only when award-standard
direction is explicitly needed; this skill is the project-specific constraint.

## Rules

- Make interaction explain hierarchy, product states, relationships, or a
  narrative handoff. If it only looks premium, omit it.
- Keep the story: identity → real products → decisions → systems → shipped
  impact → builder. The footer is a final scene, not link chrome.
- Cases prove causality and ownership, not screenshot volume. PDP Guard is the
  strongest proof of end-to-end product and engineering capability.
- Prefer one authored concept rooted in commerce, systems, audits, data, or
  engineering over generic award-site tropes (WebGL spectacle, cursors,
  smooth-scroll hijacking, arbitrary pinning, decorative grain/glass).
- Preserve readable copy, obvious navigation, keyboard/touch access,
  reduced-motion alternatives, responsive intent, and proportionate cost.

Before proposing a major idea, state what it communicates, why it belongs to
this portfolio, its interaction model, and its likely performance cost.
